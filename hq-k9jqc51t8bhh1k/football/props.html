FanDuel has 2005 plus-money props up right now (touchdown scorers, yards, catches) across 15 games. I would not bet any of them. Here is why, in plain numbers.

Why none

  • My numbers lose to FanDuel's odds. I have tracked 1055 prop bets this season. On average I said a bet would hit 51% of the time. It really hit 46%. FanDuel's odds said 44%. FanDuel was right and I was not.
  • "He has done it a lot lately" does not work either. I looked at every bet where a player had hit that number far more often in his last 10 games than the odds said (117 bets). His recent games said it would hit 82% of the time. The odds said 49%. It really hit 44%. The odds already count his recent games.
  • Plus-money bets (+300, +800) pay a lot because they usually lose. FanDuel builds a cut into every price, so over many bets the price is set for you to lose a little.
  • What would change this: if my numbers start beating FanDuel's odds over at least 100 more graded bets, this page will name the bets to pick and say why. I grade every prop FanDuel posts, every week.
